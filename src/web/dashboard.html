Primary Finding: The Relationship Between Technical Debt & Development Velocity
Methodology: Pearson correlation and simple linear regression of Technical Debt Ratio vs. a composite Development Velocity metric across a panel of 70 startups (N=120 observations). The per-company analysis addresses data independence.
